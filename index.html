POTTY CHAMP

 

How to Play

Swipe anywhere to run (arrow keys or WASD on a computer). When an alert pops up over your little brother, touch him so he follows you, then lead him to the potty before the time runs out!

Pee alert — he needs to go, fast!
Poop alert — code brown, even faster!
The potty — get him here in time to score.
Stars — every save counts toward your high score.
Hearts — you lose one for every accident.
Mop — grab it from the bathroom, then stand on a mess to clean it up and win a heart back.
Turbo — hold the shoe button (or Shift) to sprint; grab glowing shoes to refill the power bar.
⏸Pause — tap the pause button (or press P) any time.
