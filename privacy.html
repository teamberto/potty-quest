Potty Champ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Potty Champ is a game made for players of all ages, including children. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens on your device when you play.

What we store on your device

Your high score and whether you've purchased "Remove Ads" are saved only in local storage on your own device. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising

Potty Champ shows ads through Google AdMob to support free play. We've configured AdMob to treat all ad requests as child-directed: ads are not personalized based on your activity, behavioral tracking and interest-based ad targeting are turned off, and we do not use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for tracking. Google AdMob may still collect limited technical information (such as device type and approximate, non-precise location derived from IP address) needed to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's Privacy Policy for details: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Potty Champ offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. To know which devices have purchased "Remove Ads" (so we can turn ads off and let you restore the purchase later), we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, which receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous device identifier — not your name, email, or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed into that account. Because a purchase is an adult action, we show a simple math question ("parental gate") before starting a purchase or restore, so young children can't buy anything by accident.

What we don't do

We don't collect names, emails, precise location, or any other personal information. We don't have user accounts. We don't sell data to anyone. We don't run our own analytics or tracking beyond what's described above for ad delivery.

Children's privacy

Potty Champ is designed to be safe for children to play. Ads are configured for child-directed treatment as described above, and any purchase requires passing the parental gate first. If you're a parent with questions about your child's use of this app, contact us using the email below.
