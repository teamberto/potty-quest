Potty Champ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Potty Champ does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. Period.

The game runs entirely on your device and works fully offline. Your high score is saved only in local storage on your own device and never leaves it. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and no third-party services of any kind.

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either. Potty Champ is safe for all ages.
