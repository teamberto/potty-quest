**Description:**

THIS SUMMER... ONE BIG BROTHER. ONE TINY BLADDER.

Potty Champ is a fast, funny, family-friendly arcade chase. You're the
big brother, and your potty-training little brother is on the loose.
When the pee or poop alert pops up, the clock starts — catch him, lead
him through the house, and get him to the potty in time!

FEATURES

• 5 levels through an ever-bigger house — living room to full-house chaos
• Turbo mode! Hold the shoe button to sprint, grab lightning shoes to refuel
• Accidents happen — run for the mop, clean up, and win your hearts back
• Charming retro pixel art and a chiptune soundtrack
• High scores: track your best pee-and-poop rescue run
• Swipe to move — made for iPad and iPhone touch
• Free to play, with a one-time option to remove ads

Every save earns a star. A perfect run earns cake. Are you the Potty Champ?
